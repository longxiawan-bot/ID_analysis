Waqf land and solar opportunity screening

Indonesia public survey · C18 and C19 · National and provincial results

11.3% of respondents, weighted, report unused waqf land near their residence. The largest shares of nationwide weighted land reports come from West Java (25.1%), Central Java (15.7%) and East Java (10.9%).
