Waqf land and solar opportunity screening

Indonesia public survey · C18 and C19 · National and provincial results

The survey does not establish the number of unique land parcels, their area, exact location or suitability for a solar station.

From survey leads to solar sites

Use provincial results to prioritise contact with waqf managers and local communities. Build a parcel register and remove duplicate reports before counting potential sites. Record parcel boundaries, usable area, current use, manager and supporting documents.

Then assess solar resource, shading, terrain, access and grid connection, together with permissions, waqf purpose, community agreement and project economics. C18 and C19 do not measure these conditions, so no solar capacity or eligible-land total is estimated here.

Question wording and calculation method

C18: C_18. Are there any assets of waqf (e.g., land, buildings, fish ponds) that are not used/utilized around your residence?

C19: C_19. If "yes", what is the form of waqf?

Unused land = C_18=1 and at least one C_19_1–C_19_4=1 (Land). All-respondent percentages retain C18 DK/NA in the denominator. Conditional percentages use C18=1 as the denominator. Weight: w_cal, restricted to finite positive values; 0 rows excluded. Weighted share = sum of qualifying weights / sum of denominator weights. No imputation or weight renormalisation is applied.

National report share = provincial sum of land-reporter weights / nationwide sum of land-reporter weights. These shares describe reporters, not land parcels. Province field: I, using the file’s 38-province labels. This is residence geography. No unlabelled local-area fields are interpreted as parcel locations. Weight sums are not presented as land counts or population totals; the calibration population is not documented here. No confidence intervals or significance tests are supplied.
